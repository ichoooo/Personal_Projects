## 🎯 Business Questions & Objectives

### 1. Customer Strategy & Marketing Optimization
* **Churn Prediction:** Which high-value customer segments are forecasted to have the highest churn probability over the next 90 days?
* **Interventions:** What proactive marketing interventions should we launch today to alter customer lifetime value and retention?

### 2. Inventory & Markdown Management
* **Demand Forecasting:** What is the forecasted demand for seasonal apparel categories by size and color over the next quarter?
* **Markdown Strategy:** How should we structure our automated markdown timeline to liquidate slow-moving stock before its value plummets?

### 3. Supply Chain & Supplier Performance
* **Deficit Alert:** Which specific variants are predicted to face stockouts or critical inventory deficits next month?
* **Procurement Allocation:** How should we reallocate our upcoming procurement orders among top-performing vendors to prevent stockouts?

### 4. Omnichannel & Store Performance Benchmarking
* **Revenue Breakdown:** What is the total income/revenue per store location?
* **Fulfillment Expansion:** Which physical stores should we proactively plan to transition into localized omnichannel fulfillment centers?
